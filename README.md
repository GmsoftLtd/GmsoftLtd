# Grigorios Mavrogeorgis 👋

I build **free, open-source tools for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central**, mostly for manufacturing and supply chain.

Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Community **Super User** (2026 Season 1 and Season 2). Blog: [Inside Business Central](https://insidebusinesscentral.com/)

## 🧰 Free Business Central extensions

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [bc-safety-stock](https://github.com/GmsoftLtd/bc-safety-stock) | Z-score **safety stock** — *how much buffer to hold* |
| [bc-eoq-calculator](https://github.com/GmsoftLtd/bc-eoq-calculator) | Wilson **EOQ** — *how much to order* |
| [bc-reorder-point](https://github.com/GmsoftLtd/bc-reorder-point) | **Reorder point** — *when to order* |
| [bc-ValueEntryGL-Audit](https://github.com/GmsoftLtd/bc-ValueEntryGL-Audit) | Inventory-to-**G/L audit** — *do the postings tie out?* |

All MIT-licensed and built from real Item Ledger data. ⭐ Stars are welcome — they help other Business Central people find these.
